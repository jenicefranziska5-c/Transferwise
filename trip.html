Frequently asked questions

How long is each trip?

Each listed experience is planned as a seven-day trip. The exact arrival and departure times depend on the confirmed itinerary.

Is submitting the form a confirmed booking?

No. It creates a booking request. Availability and final terms must be confirmed before payment.

Can I choose my trip date?

Yes. Select your preferred date from the available dates shown on the booking form.

Are flights included?

Do not assume flights or transportation are included. Confirm the final inclusions in writing before payment.

How do I receive my booking reference?

A unique reference is generated immediately after submitting the booking request. A production website should also store this reference in a secure database and email it to the participant.

What happens after I submit?

Your request should be reviewed by the organizer. They should confirm availability, itinerary, accommodation, total price and cancellation terms before requesting payment.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation and refund conditions must be provided in the official booking agreement before payment.
